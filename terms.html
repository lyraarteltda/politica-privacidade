2. What Maestros Publisher is

Maestros Publisher is an internal application of Maestros da IA. It is the tool the company's team uses to publish videos to the YouTube channels and Instagram professional accounts owned by Maestros da IA itself, through the official Google and Meta APIs. It also reads the statistics of those accounts and public data from other Instagram professional accounts, for content research.

The app uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public.

3. Who may use it

  • The app may only be used with the YouTube channels and Instagram professional accounts owned by the company itself.
  • You may not connect other people's accounts, give access to anyone who has not been authorized, or use the app for any other purpose.
  • To use the app, you must accept these terms and the Maestros Publisher Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions the app uses

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: upload videos to the company's channels.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: read data and statistics from the company's channels.
  • instagram_business_basic: read the profile, media and metrics of the company's Instagram professional accounts.
  • instagram_business_content_publish: publish Reels to those accounts.
