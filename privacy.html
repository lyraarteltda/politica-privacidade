2. What Maestros Publisher is

Maestros Publisher is an internal application of Maestros da IA. Only the company's team uses it, to publish videos to the YouTube channels and Instagram professional accounts owned by Maestros da IA itself, through the official Google and Meta APIs.

The app is not offered to the public. No one outside the company signs in to it, and it is not used with other people's accounts.

Maestros Publisher uses YouTube API Services. For this reason, everyone who uses the app is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data the app accesses

Google and YouTube

The company authorizes access through Google OAuth 2.0, with two scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload videos to the company's channels, including the video file, title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy setting.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: to read data and statistics from the company's own channels: channel data, video list, views, likes and comment counts.

Meta and Instagram

The company authorizes access through the official Instagram login, with two permissions:

  • instagram_business_basic: to read data from the company's own professional accounts: profile, media and metrics.
  • instagram_business_content_publish: to publish Reels to those accounts.

Through Meta's Business Discovery feature, the app also reads public data from other Instagram professional accounts: username, follower count, public posts and the public metrics of those posts. We use this data only for content research.

Access tokens

The app stores the access tokens and refresh tokens issued by Google and Meta. These tokens let the app publish to and read data from the company's accounts after authorization.

What the app does not access: personal accounts, direct messages, or anyone's private data.

4. How we use the data

We use the data only to:

  • publish content to the company's own accounts;
  • measure how that content performs, in internal reports;
  • do content research with the public data from Business Discovery.

We do not:

  • sell data;
  • share data with third parties, other than the platforms listed in section 5;
  • use data for advertising or audience targeting;
  • build profiles of people;
  • use data to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

5. Who we share data with

Data goes to only two places:

  • Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram, the platforms where we publish;
  • servers we control, on infrastructure we contract to host the app.

No one else receives this data. Inside the company, only the authorized team can access it.

7. Cookies and devices

Maestros Publisher does not place cookies or use similar technologies. It does not read information from the devices of people who visit these pages.

The app does not show advertising and does not allow third parties to serve content in it. These pages have no ads, no analytics tools, no tracking pixels and no third-party content.

8. How to revoke access and request data deletion

You can remove Maestros Publisher's access at any time and ask us to delete the data. Anyone can request deletion, including the owner of an Instagram professional account whose public data we read for content research.

Google and YouTube

  1. Open the permissions page of your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. The address of the Google security settings page, https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, leads to the same place.
  2. Sign in with the Google account that authorized the app, if asked.
  3. In the list of third-party apps and services, select Maestros Publisher.
  4. Click Remove access and confirm. In some versions of the page, the button is called "Delete all connections".

Meta and Instagram

  1. On Instagram, open Settings > Apps and websites: https://www.instagram.com/accounts/manage_access/.
  2. On the Active tab, select Maestros Publisher and tap Remove.
  3. If the Instagram account is linked to a Facebook profile, also open Settings > Business integrations on Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools. Check Maestros Publisher and click Remove.

These Google and Meta pages require you to sign in. After signing in, you land on the right setting.

Deletion request by email

  1. Email contato@lyraarte.com with the subject "Data deletion: Maestros Publisher".
  2. Tell us which account it is (YouTube channel or Instagram username) and whether you want all data or only part of it deleted.
  3. We delete the data as soon as we receive the request, within 7 calendar days at most, and confirm by email when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything the app stores about that account: access and refresh tokens, any video files still on the server, statistics and metrics, and content research data.

Deadlines

  • Request by email: deletion within 7 calendar days, with confirmation by email.
  • Access removed in Google or Meta settings: we delete that account's data within 30 calendar days of the removal. We regularly check that each authorization is still valid.

Deleting the data stored by Maestros Publisher does not, in any way, affect data stored by YouTube or Instagram. Videos and Reels already published stay on the accounts until they are deleted directly on the platforms.

9. Limited Use of Google API data

Maestros Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice, data received from Google APIs is used only for the app features described in this policy. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train artificial intelligence models.

Legal basis: the legitimate interest of Maestros da IA in publishing and measuring content on its own accounts, and the operation of this service by the company itself.
